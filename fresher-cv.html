How to Write a Fresher CV With No Experience

Category: CV · 5 min read

Having no work experience doesn't mean you have nothing to show. A good fresher CV focuses on what you can do, not on what you haven't done yet.

1. Keep it to one page

Recruiters spend only a few seconds on each CV. One clean page is enough for a fresher.

2. Use this simple structure

  1. Name and contact details: phone, email, LinkedIn or GitHub link
  2. Short summary: two lines about your degree and the role you want
  3. Skills: languages, tools and software you can actually use
  4. Projects: your best two or three, with a one-line description each
  5. Education: course, institution and year
  6. Certifications: any courses you completed

3. Projects are your experience

For each project, say what you built, which tools you used, and what it does. Add a link if it's online.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Photos, long paragraphs or fancy graphics
  • Listing skills you can't explain in an interview
  • Spelling errors (read it aloud before sending)
